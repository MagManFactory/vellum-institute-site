Process

From application to a real body of work

Tell us who your student is, get matched to a cohort, take the seminar, and optionally continue with the same professor.

01

Apply

Tell us who your student is and which course interests them. It takes two minutes and does not commit you to anything.

02

Get matched to a cohort

We follow up within two business days. If it looks like a good fit, we send a full application ahead of our inaugural spring cohort.

03

Take the seminar

Eight live, ninety-minute sessions with the same small group and the same professor, ending in a substantive independent project.

04

Go further, optionally

Continue with the same professor into one-on-one guided research or apprenticeship and leave with a real body of work behind you.

Ready to start the conversation?

A parent or the student can complete the inquiry. It does not commit you to anything.
