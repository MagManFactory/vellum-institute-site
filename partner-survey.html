A few minutes of your perspective, before we lock next year’s cohorts

Vellum Institute runs small online seminars for high school students, each led by an experienced college professor and each built toward a standalone project that students complete by the end of the course. Every Fellow who wants to go further can continue with a one-on-one research project with their professor after the seminar ends.

You work directly with students weighing enrichment options, and you see more clearly than we can from the outside which courses would draw real interest and which would actually help your students. Your answers will directly shape the courses we build, how we schedule cohorts, and how we design a partnership that works for your school or organization. Thank you for taking the time to share your perspective.

This link is shared only with counselors and partner organizations directly and is not indexed or linked from our public site.

Faculty
Every seminar is led by an experienced college professor, not a recent graduate or postdoc.
Format
A real collegiate seminar, capped at a small cohort, built around genuine discussion with motivated peers.
Outcome
Each Fellow finishes the seminar with an original project they can use in their college portfolio, not a certificate of attendance.
Beyond the seminar
Students who want to go further can continue one-on-one with their professor on independent research.
Cohort timing. Vellum will offer seminar cohorts across three windows each year — spring, summer, and fall — so students can enroll around their academic calendar rather than waiting for a single annual start date.
